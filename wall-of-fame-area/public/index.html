SEO-TidY plugin required

Wall of Fame is exclusively for websites using the SEO-TidY WordPress plugin. Install and activate the plugin, then open SEO-TidY → Wall of Fame in WordPress to submit your website. Ownership is verified before moderator approval.

The Wall of Fame is an open, publicly discoverable community directory. Approved entries include your website name, address and description. Search engines and AI crawlers can visit these public listings, but indexing, traffic and recommendations are not guaranteed.
